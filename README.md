# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple calculator to calculate **Simple Interest** based on the Principal Amount, Rate of Interest, and Time Period.

## Formula

The formula used to calculate simple interest is:

**Simple Interest = (P × R × T) / 100**

Where:

* **P** = Principal Amount
* **R** = Rate of Interest
* **T** = Time Period

## Example

If:

* Principal = ₹10,000
* Rate = 5%
* Time = 2 years

Then:

**Simple Interest = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = ₹1,000**

Therefore, the **Simple Interest is ₹1,000**.
